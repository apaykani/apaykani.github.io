We develop fast, reliable computational models for energy and propulsion systems.
By combining **physics-based simulation** with **scientific machine learning**,
we study reactive flows, hydrogen and ammonia combustion, and the thermal
management of electric machines.

Led by **Dr Amin Paykani** at **Queen Mary University of London**, our group works
with academic and industrial partners to connect advances in computational
methods with the design of cleaner, more efficient technologies.
