<p class="pg-lead">Computational methods for energy and propulsion, grounded in physics and informed by data.</p>
<p class="pg-intro">We work across four connected themes, combining numerical simulation, scientific machine learning and experimental validation.</p>

<section class="pg-section pg-research" id="reactive-flows" aria-labelledby="reactive-title">
  <div>
    <h2 id="reactive-title">Reactive flows and combustion modelling</h2>
    <p>We develop high-fidelity and reduced-order models of chemically reactive flows, focusing on the interaction between turbulence, chemistry and radiation.</p>
    <p>Our work includes flame-speed measurement corrections, quasi-dimensional combustion models for fuel-blend optimisation, and non-equilibrium plasmas for combustion enhancement.</p>
    <p class="pg-small"><strong>Selected work:</strong> Faghih et al., <a href="{{ '/publications/#faghih2023cnf' | relative_url }}"><em>Combustion and Flame</em> (2023)</a>; Paykani et al., <a href="{{ '/publications/#paykani2021fuel' | relative_url }}"><em>Fuel</em> (2021)</a>.</p>

<section class="pg-section pg-research" id="scientific-ml" aria-labelledby="ml-title">
  <div>
    <h2 id="ml-title">Scientific machine learning for chemical kinetics</h2>
    <p>Detailed chemical kinetics is costly to integrate within flow simulations. We develop surrogate models that reduce this cost while retaining physical constraints and estimating prediction uncertainty.</p>
    <p>Our methods include Gaussian-process source-term integration and uncertainty-aware deep ensembles that adapt during simulation. We assess accuracy, stability and computational cost against established numerical solvers.</p>
    <p class="pg-small"><strong>Selected work:</strong> Üstün and Paykani, <a href="{{ '/publications/#ustun2025poci' | relative_url }}"><em>Proceedings of the Combustion Institute</em> (2025)</a>; <a href="{{ '/publications/#ustun2024ijhe' | relative_url }}"><em>International Journal of Hydrogen Energy</em> (2024)</a>.</p>

<section class="pg-section pg-research" id="sustainable-fuels" aria-labelledby="fuels-title">
  <div>
    <h2 id="fuels-title">Hydrogen and ammonia as fuels</h2>
    <p>We study the combustion of hydrogen, ammonia, methane and syngas blends, using chemical-kinetic analysis and data-driven models to predict burning velocities across compositions and operating conditions.</p>
    <p>Current research also investigates liquid-ammonia direct-injection sprays with Kyushu University, and the exhaust and contrail implications of cracked ammonia for aviation.</p>
    <p class="pg-small"><strong>Selected work:</strong> Mashruk et al., <a href="{{ '/publications/#mashruk2024ef' | relative_url }}"><em>Energy &amp; Fuels</em> (2024)</a>; Üstün et al., <a href="{{ '/publications/#ustun2024fuel' | relative_url }}"><em>Fuel</em> (2024)</a>; Paykani et al., <a href="{{ '/publications/#paykani2022pecs' | relative_url }}"><em>Progress in Energy and Combustion Science</em> (2022)</a>.</p>

<section class="pg-section pg-research" id="thermal-management" aria-labelledby="thermal-title">
  <div>
    <h2 id="thermal-title">Thermal management of electric machines</h2>
    <p>Heat removal is an important constraint on the power density of electric traction motors. We investigate direct oil-jet cooling of hairpin end-windings through CFD and experiments.</p>
    <p>We examine nozzle geometry, flow rate and impingement angle, including multi-nozzle configurations, to guide cooling-system design with industrial partners.</p>
    <p class="pg-small"><strong>Selected work:</strong> Maddumage et al., <a href="{{ '/publications/#maddumage2025ieee' | relative_url }}"><em>IEEE Transactions on Transportation Electrification</em> (2025)</a>; <a href="{{ '/publications/#maddumage2025ate' | relative_url }}"><em>Applied Thermal Engineering</em> (2025)</a>.</p>
