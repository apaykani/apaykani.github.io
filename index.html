Paykani Research Group

Scientific AI & Computational Engineering for Energy and Propulsion
School of Engineering and Materials Science · Queen Mary University of London

We combine physics-based simulation and scientific machine learning to study energy and propulsion systems.

Our research spans reactive flows, hydrogen and ammonia combustion, and the cooling of electric machines. We develop computational methods and test them against experiments, working with academic and industrial partners.

Research themes

Reactive flows and combustion

Models of turbulence, chemistry and radiation, including plasma-assisted combustion.

Scientific machine learning

Efficient models for stiff chemical kinetics, with physical constraints and uncertainty estimates.

Hydrogen and ammonia

Combustion chemistry and spray behaviour for engines, turbines and aviation.

Thermal management

Cooling of electric machines through CFD, experiments and design optimisation.
