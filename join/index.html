PhD opportunities

Quantum Computing and AI for Nonlinear Dynamics

Applications open — QMUL–China Scholarship Council scholarship route

This PhD will investigate physics-informed AI and hybrid quantum-classical methods for simulating nonlinear dynamical systems. Chemical reaction kinetics, including hydrogen and ammonia combustion, will provide challenging test problems for assessing accuracy, stability and computational efficiency.

  • Expected start: September 2027
  • Application deadline: 27 January 2027, 16:00 GMT
  • Funding: Queen Mary tuition-fee support and CSC support for living expenses, subject to securing a CSC scholarship
  • Eligibility: Chinese applicants meeting the QMUL–CSC scheme requirements
  • Application reference: SEMS-PHD-738

View the full project description and application instructions.

For informal enquiries, contact Dr Amin Paykani.

Machine Learning-Driven Thermal Optimisation for High-Power-Density Electric Motors

Forthcoming EPSRC IDLA PhD studentship — expressions of interest welcome

This project, in collaboration with the Dyson Institute, will investigate how machine learning and computational modelling can improve thermal design and cooling strategies for compact, high-power-density electric motors.

The research connects scientific machine learning, thermal-fluid modelling and engineering design, with opportunities to address challenges relevant to industrial motor development.

  • Expected start: October 2027
  • Studentship scheme: EPSRC Industrial Doctoral Landscape Award (IDLA)
  • Industrial collaboration: Dyson Institute
  • Status: Formal advertisement and final funding details to follow

We welcome early enquiries from candidates with interests in heat transfer, computational fluid dynamics, electric machines, machine learning or engineering optimisation.

To express interest, email a.paykani@qmul.ac.uk with your CV and a short explanation of your relevant experience and interests.

Postdoctoral researchers

Funded PDRA positions are advertised above and on the QMUL jobs portal.

  • Royal Society Newton International Fellowship

Fellowship applications are substantial undertakings. Please get in touch well ahead of the deadline so there is time to shape the proposal properly.

Visiting researchers

We host visiting PhD students and academics for periods of three to twelve months. Visitors are expected to arrive with their own funding and to have a clear research question that connects to one of our themes.

How to get in touch

Email me at a.paykani@qmul.ac.uk, including:

  1. A short statement of your research interests
  2. Your CV
  3. Which of our research themes you would like to work in, and why

The third point is the one that matters. Enquiries that could have been sent to any group are unlikely to receive a reply.
